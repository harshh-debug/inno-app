Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Innogeeks account and associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the app, go to Settings > Account, and tap Delete Account.
  2. Or email innogeeks.kiet@edu from your registered email address with the subject "Delete My Account".

What gets deleted

All your personal data, including profile information, messages, and any other content associated with your account, will be deleted.

Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified request. {{RETENTION_EXCEPTIONS}}

Questions? Contact us at innogeeks.kiet@edu.
